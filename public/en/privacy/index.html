2. Data we process

Depending on the features used, we may process:

  • Account data: name, username, email, and password stored as a hash.
  • Profile data: photo, cover image, bio, location, website, interests, and privacy settings.
  • User-generated content: posts, photos, videos, Stories, comments, messages, and reposts.
  • Social relationships: followers, friendships, requests, blocks, and mutes.
  • Interactions: likes, saved items, views, mentions, activity, and signals used for recommendations.
  • Security and moderation data: reports, restrictions, suspensions, and administrative actions.
  • Technical and security data, such as identifiers derived from the IP address, browser type, device, date/time, access attempts, and events necessary to prevent abuse and fraud and maintain the service.
  • First-party campaign attribution: when a person arrives through a campaign link, we may associate that campaign with their registration and aggregate visits, challenge starts, and sharing actions to measure the launch. This measurement does not use third-party advertising cookies.
