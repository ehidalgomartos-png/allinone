Community Guidelines

Instant Admirers is an 18+ community for connecting, sharing, and discovering with respect.

Version 1.020 September 202618+

This English version is provided for convenience. The Spanish version is the reference version.

1. Respect and safety

Debate, disagreement, creativity, and expression are allowed. Credible threats, targeted harassment, persistent abuse, doxxing, and coordinated abuse campaigns are not allowed.

2. Hate and discrimination

Content that attacks, dehumanises, or promotes violence or discrimination against people or groups based on characteristics protected by applicable law is not allowed.

3. Minors

Instant Admirers is exclusively for adults aged 18 and over. Any exploitation, abuse, or sexualisation of minors, as well as grooming or recruitment behaviour, is strictly prohibited.

4. Sexual and intimate content

Pornography and explicit sexual content are not allowed in this version of Instant Admirers. Sharing intimate images without consent, sextortion, voyeurism, sexually manipulated material depicting a real person without authorisation, and any illegal sexual content are also prohibited.

5. Violence and dangerous conduct

Promoting real-world violence, serious threats, terrorism, instructions to cause serious harm, or targeted glorification of harm against people is not allowed.

6. Fraud, impersonation, and spam

Do not deceptively impersonate others, steal credentials, scam people, distribute malware, or artificially manipulate followers, likes, comments, or other metrics. Abusive automation and repetitive spam may lead to restrictions.

7. Intellectual property and privacy

Respect copyright, trademarks, honour, privacy, image rights, and data-protection rights. Do not publish third-party private data in a harmful way or without a legitimate basis.

8. Moderation and repeat violations

Measures may include content removal, warnings, feature limitations, temporary suspension, or account closure. Severity, risk, context, and repeat behaviour influence the response. Repeated abuse of the reporting system may also lead to measures.

9. How to report

Use Report on the relevant profile or post. For potentially illegal content, you can also email vrmatch.es@gmail.com with the information needed to locate and assess the content.
